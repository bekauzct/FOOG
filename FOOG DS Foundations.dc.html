01 — Color

Brand core, mapped to two themes

Volt is the signal — primary actions, live states and brand moments. Keep it under ~10% of any screen. Midnight carries the product. Signal Blue is for links, focus and info. Dark is the default app theme; light is used for web, booking receipts and partner tools.

Archivo for voice, JetBrains Mono for data

07 — Iconography
{{ i }}
  • Material Symbols Rounded — rounded terminals match the OO's curves. Weight 400, optical size 24.
  • Outlined by default; filled only for the active/selected state (nav, like, save).
  • Sizes 16 / 20 / 24 / 32. Never decorative — every icon pairs with an action or a label.
08 — Imagery
{{ r.label }}
{{ r.name }}
  • Real players, real pitches. Natural light or floodlit night; slightly cool grade, true blacks.
  • No stock handshakes, no generic ball close-ups. People in motion > empty stadiums.
  • Text over media sits on a bottom scrim (midnight 0→70%), never on raw photo.
09 — Motion
{{ m.token }}{{ m.v }}
{{ m.use }}
Fast, not flashy. UI responds in under 120ms; nothing blocks input.
Kick, then settle. Positive moments (like, join, booked) get one quick overshoot — never loops.
Spatial continuity. Cards expand into detail; sheets rise from the bottom; nothing teleports.
Respect reduced motion. Swap movement for 120ms fades.
